    Getting started

    1. Plug the charger into a wall socket.
    2. Connect a USB-C cable to the charger and to your device.
    3. Charging starts automatically. Unplug the charger when the device is full.

    Safety

    • Use indoors only and keep it dry.
    • Stop using it and contact support if it becomes hot, damaged or smells of burning.
    • Do not use it to charge a laptop that needs more than 20 W.

    User guide

    The VC-20 charger negotiates the best charging speed with your device automatically.

    If charging seems slow, try a different cable. Cables that carry only data cannot charge at full speed.

    Clean the charger with a dry cloth only.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can it charge a laptop?

    Only laptops that charge at 20 W or less. Most laptops need 45 W to 65 W, which this charger cannot provide.

    How do I claim the warranty?

    Keep your purchase receipt and contact support with the model number VC-20. The warranty lasts 12 months from purchase.
