    Safety

    • Never open the lid while a job is running.
    • Wear laser safety glasses when servicing with the lid open.
    • Keep a fire extinguisher within reach of the machine.
    • Do not cut PVC or vinyl: it releases toxic gas.

    Operating guide

    Switch on the exhaust fan first, then the machine. Wait for the display to show Ready before loading a job.

    Place the material flat on the bed and set the focus using the gauge. Close the lid before starting.

    At the end of the day, switch off the machine first and the exhaust fan last, and remove all offcuts from the bed.
